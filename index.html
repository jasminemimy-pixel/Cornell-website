About Us

Welcome to the Cornell University Department of ICT. We teach students about programming, databases, computer networks and web development. Our lecturers help students learn by doing, and most classes include practical work in our computer laboratory. After finishing their studies, our students are ready to work in the IT industry or continue to further studies.

  • Database Systems
  • Computer Networks
  • Web Application Development
  • Cybersecurity Fundamentals
